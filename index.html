Welcome here!
I am BetaNeutrino, thats my developer name, obviously thats not my real name! I honestly don't know whether or not I want to make this my actual identity or my company identity (because I do plan to start an organization), after some careful planning, I've decided I might decide to keep this name for now, and in the future - if I ever seriously consider register it as an organization, I will move on to my old username for my personal stuff, and rebrand BetaNeutrino - or, start the formal stuff from GammaNeutrino.

Although that exactly doesn't describe who I am, let me explain who I am - Honestly, I don't know either. I am just a software developer who fricking loves computers but has no clear goal or idea on what to do with the computer! so I pretty much know a lot of stuff - Bash, C, Ricing (The term in UNIX Communities to customize ones setup deeply), Graphics, Editing stuff - you name it.

One thing is very clear with how far I've came tho, I don't really care about doing things for the sake of a portfolio or modern work culture, I just do them because I like it. Seriously. I hate modern software culture where developers would learn something purely for the sake of professional job opportunities rather than the old spirit of using computers.

This site is only made with simple HTML and CSS too - 2000s philosophy of web development, or I'd say the UNIX Philosophy? who knows, however you put it(Although, I do follow the UNIX Philosophy somewhat religiously for my core software, so it might bleed!). Its a protest(sorta?) against how most modern personal portfolios or personal blog sites have like a shit-ton of React or JavaScript bullshit, Honestly I might be illiterate on this topic, but for a reason I know its unnecessary bloat or eye candy. A site should be easily navigable, and should be easy to the eye, and more importantly - a site should ABSOLUTELY NOT, under any circumstance require a beefy device to run. Anyway, I think I am making my point pretty clear that I won't be making this site an React-junk (or you know). I might write a blog regarding this, so feel free to read it sometime.
